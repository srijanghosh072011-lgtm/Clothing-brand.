## Notes

### Don't make it monthly-on-a-date

You said "every month or so" — keep the *or so*. A discount that lands on the
1st of every month teaches your list to wait for the 1st. At $75–$245 a piece,
training customers out of full price is expensive: 10% off on a $185 hoodie is
$18.50 of pure margin, every time, including on people who would have paid full.

**5–6 weeks, varied, is the pattern that works.** It stays a reason to open
without becoming a schedule people plan around.

### Use a fresh code each send

Not `WELCOME10`. Generate a new one per campaign — `NOCT-OCT`, `NOCT-NOV` —
with a **7-day expiry set in Shopify**. Two reasons:

1. The deadline is real, so the urgency in the copy is honest.
2. You can see exactly which campaign drove which orders.

Set the expiry on the discount itself, not just in the copy. A code that still
works after the stated deadline makes every future deadline meaningless.

### Suppression

Exclude anyone who ordered in the last 14 days. Nobody wants a discount code
three days after paying full price for the same thing.
